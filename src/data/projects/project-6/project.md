## Overview

AI is often designed to answer questions. But some of the most important questions in life don't have clear answers.

What does this relationship mean to me? Why does this situation keep bothering me? What am I actually looking for? Am I changing, or repeating the same patterns?

**AI Readings** explores a different role for AI: not as a source of answers, but as a tool for interpretation.

A reading takes something happening in your life—a challenge, ambition, relationship, decision, or emotion—and helps you look at it from a different perspective. Rather than telling you what to do, it gives you a new lens through which to understand what you're experiencing.

Over time, these readings form a personal collection. Individual moments begin to connect: recurring concerns, changing priorities, emotional patterns, and shifts in how you see yourself. What starts as reflection on a single moment can gradually become a record of how you've changed.

## Why Astrology?

The first version of AI Readings uses astrology as its interpretive framework.

Astrology is expressive, visual, and inherently personal. Its planets, houses, aspects, and transits provide a rich symbolic language for talking about relationships, ambition, conflict, uncertainty, and emotion.

It also makes reflection feel less like filling out a psychological questionnaire and more like exploration.

The goal isn't simply to digitize a traditional horoscope. It's to use AI to turn these symbolic systems into something interactive—something you can explore, respond to, and return to as your life changes.

## Making Reflection an Experience

Most tools for self-reflection are built around text: journals, questionnaires, chat interfaces, or long-form reports.

AI Readings experiments with a different form.

Ideas become visual and interactive. Different perspectives can be explored rather than compressed into a single answer. Readings can be saved and revisited, allowing reflection to accumulate instead of disappearing at the end of a conversation.

The broader question behind the product is simple:

**What if AI could help us not only find information, but make sense of our own experiences?**

The current astrology-based prototype is an early exploration of that idea.
